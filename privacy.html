Privacy Policy – Free Food Scan

Last updated: 4 November 2026

The short version

Free Food Scan does not have accounts, does not collect your personal data, and does not track you. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What stays on your device

  • Your scan history, diet list and "About you" details (age, sex, height, weight, activity level).
  • Your number of free scans used and whether you've unlocked the app.

These are stored in your browser's local storage on your device only. We never receive them. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes them.

Camera

The camera is only used to read barcodes, and only while the scanner is open. Images are processed on your device and are never uploaded or saved.

Product lookups

When you scan a product, its barcode number (and nothing else about you) is sent to these public product databases to fetch its details:

  • Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts and Open Pet Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org)
  • USDA FoodData Central (api.nal.usda.gov)
  • UPCitemdb (upcitemdb.com)

Like any website, these services may see your device's IP address when they respond. Their own privacy policies apply.

Payments

If you buy the unlimited-scans unlock, payment is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Health information

Scores and guidance in the app are general information, not medical or dietary advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
